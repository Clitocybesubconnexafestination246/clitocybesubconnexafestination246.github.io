👋 Overview

Binaural Studio acts as a digital workspace for sound designers and casual listeners. You can create complex audio environments for study, relaxation, or deep work. The software mixes binaural tones with your own music files and spoken-word recordings. It runs entirely inside your web browser. You do not need to install complex software or manage internet accounts.

🎹 Features

The application includes several tools to help you build your custom audio mix:

  • Binaural Synthesizer: Ten independent voices allow you to create specific beat frequencies between 1 and 40 Hz.
  • Music Deck: Import your local music files to provide a background track. The software performs real-time analysis to ensure the binaural tones match the key of your music.
  • Spoken-Word Deck: A dedicated player for audiobooks or podcasts. This keeps your voice audio separate from your music tracks.
  • Preset Engine: Save your session setups and switch between them while you listen.
  • Master Recorder: Capture your entire mix as a single high-quality audio file for later use.

🚀 Getting Started

Using Binaural Studio does not require a traditional installation process because it runs directly through your web browser. Follow these steps to begin:

  1. Ensure you have a pair of stereo headphones. Binaural audio relies on the separation of sound between your left and right ears.
  2. Open your preferred web browser, such as Chrome, Firefox, or Edge.

💻 System Requirements

Because the application runs in your browser, it works on almost any modern computer. We recommend a version of Windows 10 or 11. Your computer should have at least 8 GB of RAM for smooth audio processing. Ensure your browser is up to date to maintain full compatibility with the audio engine.

⚙️ Usage Tips

Start your session at a comfortable volume level to protect your hearing. Since everyone perceives sound differently, adjust the tone frequencies until you find a setting that suits your needs. You can experiment with the sine wave settings to change the texture of the sound.

🔒 Privacy and Local Storage

This software uses a local-first design. All audio playback and analysis happen on your own computer. The software sends no audio files to a server, and you do not need to create an account. Your work remains private.
